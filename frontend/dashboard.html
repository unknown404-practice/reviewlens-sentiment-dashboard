About ReviewLens Intelligence Platform

Architecture & Governance

1. What is ReviewLens?

ReviewLens is an enterprise-grade NLP sentiment intelligence platform designed to ingest e-commerce customer reviews, perform data quality auditing and text cleaning, extract granular linguistic features, and execute rule-based sentiment analytics. It bridges exploratory data science into production microservices and executive reporting dashboards.

2. End-to-End Architecture

01 Data Pipeline Jupyter notebooks, schema validation, synthetic generator, text sanitization.
→
02 VADER Engine Polarity scores, 3-class classification, 5-tier intensity segmentation.
→
03 FastAPI Service Typed Pydantic endpoints, in-memory caching, anti-SSRF checks, SlowAPI limits.
→
04 Streamlit Dashboard Vanilla HTML/CSS/JS frontend embedded in a responsive Streamlit iframe.

3. VADER Score Interpretation Thresholds

VADER (Valence Aware Dictionary and sEntiment Reasoner) scores text by summing the lexical valence of each recognized token, adjusted by heuristical rules (punctuation, capitalization, negation, degree adverbs), normalized to a compound score between -1.0 and +1.0.

Compound Score Range Sentiment Classification Granular Intensity Tier
compound >= 0.60 Positive Strong Positive
0.05 <= compound < 0.60 Positive Positive
-0.05 < compound < 0.05 Neutral Neutral
-0.60 < compound <= -0.05 Negative Negative
compound <= -0.60 Negative Strong Negative

4. Data Transparency & Ethical Collection

The demonstration dataset provided with ReviewLens consists of deterministically generated synthetic customer reviews created using Python's pseudo-random number generator (seed = 42). It does not represent unpermitted scraped data from Amazon, Flipkart, or any third-party marketplace. ReviewLens adheres strictly to responsible web scraping guidelines: it operates only on permitted public endpoints and never bypasses authentication, CAPTCHAs, or rate limits.

5. Methodological Limitations

  • Social Lexicon Bias: VADER was calibrated on social media text; subtle hardware specifications or specialized vocabulary may produce skewed valence.
  • Sarcasm and Subtlety: Complex conditional clauses or nuanced product trade-offs remain challenging for rule-based systems.
  • Star Rating vs. Sentiment Disconnect: Polite customer language in 3-star reviews can trigger positive valence tokens despite mediocre satisfaction.
